Privacy policy

ParisMood Shorts – last updated 25 November 2026

ParisMood Shorts is a private tool used only by the owner of the ParisMood YouTube channel to manage their own content. With the owner's explicit permission (Google sign-in), it accesses:

  • Google Drive: to read, organise and save the owner's own video clips and finished videos in a dedicated folder.
  • YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: to read the channel's own videos and statistics, and to update the titles and descriptions of the channel's own videos.

This data is used only to produce and manage the channel's videos. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. Access tokens are stored only in the owner's private environment.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: through the ParisMood YouTube channel.
